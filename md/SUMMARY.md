# ScaleX LaunchPad — Progress Update

**July 2026**

---

## What we are building

ScaleX LaunchPad is your online academy for Amazon FBA students. It takes someone from complete beginner to a launched seller through structured lessons, real assignments, mentor feedback, and community support.

Students use the **LaunchPad** to learn and grow.  
Your team uses the **Admin area** to run the academy day to day.

---

## Where we are now

**The core student experience is live and working.**

Students can sign up, pay, log in, work through the full 8-milestone Amazon roadmap, submit their assignments, get mentor feedback, earn badges, use the AI mentor, join the community, and sign up for live classes.

Your team can log in, review student work, moderate the community, and schedule live sessions.

**What comes next** is the business side — sales tracking, finance, full student management, and richer reporting dashboards.

**Mobile apps** for iPhone and Android are planned after that.

---

## What students get today

- A branded sign-up and payment experience
- A personal dashboard showing where they are on their Amazon journey
- All 8 milestones from Foundation to Scaling, with lessons inside each one
- The ability to submit real work at the end of each milestone — business plans, product sheets, launch checklists, and more
- An AI assistant they can ask questions anytime
- Badges and levels as they progress — from Beginner Seller to Amazon Launcher
- A community to share wins, ask questions, and help each other
- Live classes and access to recordings afterward
- Alerts when their work has been reviewed or they unlock something new

---

## What your team gets today

- A secure login for admins, instructors, mentors, and sales staff
- A queue to review student submissions and give feedback
- AI-assisted notes on each submission to speed up mentor reviews — mentors still approve everything personally
- Tools to approve or send work back for revision
- Community moderation so posts are reviewed before going public
- Scheduling for live classes, Q&A sessions, and masterclasses
- A home screen showing what needs attention right now

---

## How a student moves through the program

1. They sign up and pay to join
2. They open their dashboard and start the roadmap
3. They complete lessons milestone by milestone
4. They submit their assignment for that milestone
5. A mentor reviews it and gives feedback
6. Once approved, they earn their badge, level up, and the next milestone opens
